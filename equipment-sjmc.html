SJMC Equipment

Cameras, recorders, microphones, lights, and other equipment that SJMC lends to students, and which classes can borrow each item.

To borrow equipment, see Checking out equipment. It explains how to reserve, pick up, and return gear, and the rules.

What you can borrow depends on the class you are taking. Find your class first, then read about each item below. Counts are the units in working order in November 2026. Some may be checked out or in repair when you ask.

Drones

SJMC has 12 DJI Mini 3 Pro drones. Each comes with a remote control, micro SD card, three batteries, a charger, propeller guards, spare blades, and cables.

Drones are for students with a drone license only, and only in semesters when the drone class is not offered.
