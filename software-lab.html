SJMC Software

Software installed in SJMC labs, how to get each one, and links to its manuals and tutorials.

Getting the software

Most of this software is on the lab computers in the SJMC labs and in the Student Center.

Some software asks you to sign in with your HawkID.

If you want to work on your own computer, each program below says whether it is free or needs a purchase, and how to get it.

To come: the lab room numbers, Student Center location, and lab hours.
