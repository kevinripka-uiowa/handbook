SJMC Production Handbook

What professionals know about making media: files, equipment, software settings, and the standards behind them.

Why this handbook

There are more ways to make media than ever. Templates, phone apps, and AI can turn out a poster, a video, or a podcast in minutes. You'll use some of them at work. Plenty of professionals make social graphics in Canva.

Easy tools make technical choices for you: resolution, color, file format, frame rate, loudness. Often they choose well. When they don't, the poster prints blurry, the video won't upload, the colors shift, or the podcast is too quiet, and someone has to know why.

That's what this handbook covers. Whatever tool you use, easy or professional, you should be able to tell whether it's making and exporting the right thing for where the audience will see it.
